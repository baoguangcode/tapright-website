Our story

Why I Built TapRight

When our daughter, Selena, was born in January 2026, my perspective changed overnight. Parenthood has been more rewarding than I ever imagined, but it also made my wife and me much more mindful of every dollar as our family grows.

I've been into credit card rewards and loyalty perks for years, and friends have always asked me how to maximize their spending. I wanted to turn what I've learned into something simple that anyone could use.

Building TapRight hasn't been easy to juggle. I'm not a software engineer, and switching over to building an app was a steep learning curve. Most of it came together in small pockets of time, on work breaks and after the baby finally fell asleep. Modern AI helped me bridge the gap to make it happen, and I poured a lot of care into the design and details along the way.

As for the data, I know how frustrating it is to rely on outdated information. That's why every reward rate in the app is checked directly against official bank terms and clearly stamped with the date it was verified, so you can always see where the information comes from and how current it is.

TapRight is very much a work in progress. If you spot a mistake or have an idea to make it better, I'd truly love to hear from you.

What's in a Name?

TapRight is published by Thousand Moons. Our daughter's Chinese name, 千月, translates to "a thousand moons", roughly the span of a human lifetime. This project is built with her in mind.

Transparency & How This App Makes Money

Finding the right card to tap is 100% free and always will be.

  • Affiliate links: If you decide to apply for a card through the app, I may earn a small referral commission from the bank. This never changes how cards are ranked or recommended.
  • Tips: If TapRight ends up saving you money, you'll also have the option to leave a tip to support its ongoing development.
