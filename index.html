Our story

Why I Built TapRight

When our daughter, Selena, was born in January 2026, my perspective changed overnight. Parenthood brought incredible joy, but it also made my wife and me hyper-focused on stretching our family's budget.

I've spent years optimizing credit card rewards, bank bonuses, and loyalty perks. Friends and family were always asking me how to maximize their spending, so I decided to build a tool that makes it effortless for everyone.

Building TapRight meant squeezing work into spare moments: on lunch breaks, after bedtime, and on quiet weekends. I'm not a software engineer by trade, but modern AI tools helped bridge the gap from concept to code.

What I do bring from my background in aerospace quality is relentless precision. In this field, "close enough" isn't an option. Every reward rate in TapRight is verified directly against official bank terms and stamped with the date it was checked, so you can see exactly how current it is.

TapRight is an honest work in progress. If you spot an error or have an idea to make it better, I'd love to hear from you.

What's in a Name?

TapRight is published by Thousand Moons. Our daughter's Chinese name, 千月, translates to "a thousand moons", roughly the span of a human lifetime. Thousand Moons is named for her, and built for her future.

Transparency & How This App Makes Money

Finding the right card to tap is 100% free and always will be.

  • Affiliate links: If you apply for a card through TapRight, I may earn a small referral commission from the bank. This never impacts card rankings or recommendations.
  • Tips: If TapRight saves you money or earns you extra points, you have the option to leave a tip to support ongoing development.
