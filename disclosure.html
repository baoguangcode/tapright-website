How TapRight makes money

Last updated: October 29, 2026

TapRight is free. Rankings are never for sale.

Referral commissions (planned)

In the future, TapRight may earn a referral commission from a bank when someone applies for a card through a link in the app. The app has no such links today.

Rankings are never for sale

Commissions will never affect rankings. Which card comes first, and which cards are suggested, is based only on rewards value. A card is never moved up because it pays a commission, and never left out because it doesn't.

Tips

Tips are optional and help cover the cost of running TapRight. Tipping doesn't unlock anything or change what the app shows.

Independent

TapRight is made by Baoguang Xu (Thousand Moons), an independent developer in Ontario, Canada. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any bank or card issuer.
